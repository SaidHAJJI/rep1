Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae, and some bacteria use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to create their own food in the form of glucose (a sugar) and release oxygen as a byproduct. This process is crucial for life on Earth as it produces most of the oxygen in the atmosphere. It primarily occurs in the chloroplasts, which are specialized organelles found in plant cells. Chlorophyll, a green pigment within the chloroplasts, is responsible for capturing light energy. The overall chemical equation for photosynthesis is 6CO2 + 6H2O + Light Energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2.
